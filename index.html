About me

I am visual designer working for over a decade for my own studio, Signified.

With extensive experience in web and digital interfaces design, I aim to bridge the gap between user needs, information clarity and tasteful nuanced presentation. Throughout the years, I have led the design for digital platforms, editorial layouts, and public-facing campaigns. My process is uniquely informed by social science research and cultural insight, allowing me to build digital products that are are visually compelling and deeply rooted in an psychological and cultural understanding of user behaviour.

I find the most fulfilment in partnering with organisations where I can apply my expertise to support causes that prioritise social value.
